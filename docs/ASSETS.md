# Third-party assets

Every asset in this repo that was not made for it, with where it came from, its licence and what it is used for.
Original game content is never listed here because it is never committed (see README.md).

## Surfaces and sky (Tools/Import, Content/OpenSCCT/ThirdParty)

All of these are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication): no attribution is required, it is given anyway.
`Tools/Import/pbr_sets.json` is the machine-readable copy of this list (exact download URLs);
`Tools/Import/Import-Assets.ps1` downloads, packs and imports them.

`T_<Set>_N` (its DirectX normal map) and `T_<Set>_ORD` (R ambient occlusion, G roughness re-centred on 0.5,

## The merc's rifle (Art/Weapons/MercRifle, Content/OpenSCCT/Weapons)

The Halvard HV-68 "Warden" is our own work: a fictional bullpup carbine with fictional markings, modelled, unwrapped,
textured and rigged by the scripts in `Art/Weapons/MercRifle/` (`Build-MercRifle.ps1` runs them) and imported by
`Tools/Import/Import-MercRifle.ps1`. Its geometry, its four textures and everything under
`Content/OpenSCCT/Weapons/` are generated there; nothing in them is downloaded or taken from the original game.
The scripts lean on two things that ship with Blender 5.2 and are not copied into this repo:

| Item | Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inter (variable font, `datafiles/fonts/Inter.woff2`) | https://rsms.me/inter/ (bundled with Blender) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | the lettering painted into the rifle's textures (maker's plate, serial, selector marks, labels) |
| `studio.exr` (`datafiles/studiolights/world/`) | bundled with Blender | CC0 1.0 | the environment the beauty renders in `Art/Weapons/MercRifle/renders/` reflect; not used by the game |

"Halvard Defense Systems", "HV-68 Warden", "HX-2 Holo" and the 6.8x43 calibre are invented for this game.

## Effects (Tools/Import, Content/OpenSCCT/Effects)

Nothing here was downloaded. `Content/OpenSCCT/Effects/` holds only things built by this repo's own scripts
(`Tools/Import/Import-Effects.ps1`):

| Asset | Made by | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| M_ScctParticleLit, M_ScctParticleGlow | `scct_effects.py` | sprite particles (smoke and steam lit by the room; sparks, flames, bubbles) |
| M_ScctCorona | `scct_effects.py` | lamp halos (camera-facing cards that fade when the lamp is hidden) |
| M_ScctLightFunction | `scct_effects.py` | projector-light rectangles and pictures, caustics, pulsing and flickering lights |
| M_ScctProjector | `scct_effects.py` | projected signs, graffiti and rain splashes (decals) |
| M_ScctGlowCard | `scct_effects.py` | the original's modelled beam and flare cards |
| NS_ScctSprites | `scct_niagara.py` (through the engine's Niagara toolset) | every sprite emitter and rain volume; built from the engine's own Niagara modules |
| M_ScctWet | `scct_effects.py` | wet, shining ground with standing water under rain volumes (a decal that changes roughness only; its patches are computed noise, no texture) |
| T_RainStreak | `make_effect_textures.py` (drawn with numpy) | a raindrop streak |

The textures the placed effects show (smoke, sparks, coronas, cookies) are original game content: they are
imported per map into `Content/Imported/` and never committed. A release cooks the imported maps, and with them
these textures, into its paks (docs/RELEASING.md): bundled in releases, never committed.

## Pawn skins (Tools/Materials, Content/OpenSCCT/Materials/PawnSkins)

The state skins `Source/OpenSCCT/Play/ScctPawnSkins` draws over a pawn's body are materials built by this repo's own
script (`Tools/Materials/Build-PawnSkinMaterials.ps1`, which runs `build_pawn_skin_materials.py`):

| Asset | Used for |
|---|---|
| M_ScctStickySkin | the sticky shocker's Stickess skin |
| M_ScctShockImmunityEdge | the merc's shock-protection edge (a computed fresnel edge, no texture) |
| M_ScctWetSkin, MI_ScctWetSkin_ATT, MI_ScctWetSkin_ATT2, MI_ScctWetSkin_DEF | the wet skin of a pawn in the rain (spy body, spy head, merc body) |

The textures these skins show (the Stickess and wet-skin textures of SPersoTextures) are original game content. They
are not committed: the game takes them at run time from the player's own imported content.
